Thomas Nellen

Economic Advisor · Swiss National Bank · Financial Stability, Stress Testing and Data Science
Economic Advisor · Study Center Gerzensee

I am an economic advisor at the Swiss National Bank, working on stress testing and data science within financial stability, and at the Study Center Gerzensee. My research is in payment economics, the design of financial market infrastructures, financial intermediation, money markets and monetary policy implementation.

I serve as co-organizer and/or scientific committee member for the Economics of Payments conference, the BIS–CEPR–Gerzensee–SFI Financial Intermediation Conference and the Summer Workshop on Money, Banking, Payments and Finance.
