Senior Research Advisor · Study Center Gerzensee · Programme Manager, Advanced Central Bank Courses

I am an economic advisor at the Swiss National Bank, working on stress testing and data science within financial stability, and senior research advisor at the Study Center Gerzensee. My research is in payment economics and the design of financial market infrastructures, and on how money markets and monetary policy implementation depend on the plumbing beneath them. Recent work concerns settlement liquidity, central queuing arrangements, and the use of transaction data to detect bank stress early.
