Experience

since 2011

Economic Advisor, Swiss National Bank — Oversight, Financial Stability, Zurich

Leads policy and regulation, covering the revisions of the National Bank Ordinance and the Financial Market Infrastructure Act, the Swiss–UK mutual recognition agreement, and the application of the PFMI to stablecoin arrangements. Deputy CPMI member, member of the CPMI–IOSCO Steering Group and the Policy Standing Group. Systemic importance assessments for LCH, Eurex Clearing, SIX Digital Exchange, Fnality and Libra. Leads four data science proofs of concept on bank liquidity monitoring, settlement liquidity, repo market access and bank stress early warning.
